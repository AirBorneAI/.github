## 🌌 Overview & Mission

Modern enterprise organizations require agile, data-driven, and resilient workforce operating systems. At **AirBorne HRS**, we engineer cloud-native architectures and proprietary AI pipelines that streamline the entire talent lifecycle:

- 🎯 **Algorithmic Talent Matching**: Zero-shot semantic skill embedding search and multidimensional candidate graph modeling.
- ⚡ **High-Throughput HRMS Core**: Resilient transactional engines handling complex organizational hierarchies, dynamic payroll execution, and continuous compliance.
- 📈 **Predictive Workforce Telemetry**: Deep statistical insights, attrition forecasting, and real-time team capability mapping.
- 🌐 **Global Recruitment Pipeline**: Frictionless candidate pipelines, automated screening bots, and collaborative assessment workspaces.
